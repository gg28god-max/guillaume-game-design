09 — DESIGN DISCIPLINE

The Four-Phase Production Protocol

To ensure that a collection spanning vehicles, heavy robots, humanoid characters, and creatures remains visually coherent, every asset passes through the exact same four-step production gate.

PHASE 01

Silhouette Grammar

Pure black/white thumbnail passes testing whether the unit reads instantly from afar before a single highlight or seam is drawn.

PHASE 02

Functional Logic

Orthographic blueprints establishing hydraulic clearances, pressurized hatch access, saddle rigging straps, and weapon hardpoints.

PHASE 03

Material Differentiation

Establishing distinct specular and roughness behaviors between marine titanium, weathered skin, plumage, and optical polymer domes.

PHASE 04

Modular Variants

Testing practical uniform colorways, safety vs. stealth liveries, and military vs. pack configurations for gameplay modularity.
