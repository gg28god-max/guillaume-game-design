  • ▸

  • ▸

  • ▸
    Keyframes & Color Scripts: Cinematic storytelling moments, lighting studies, and atmosphere exploration.

2. 3D Modeling & Digital Sculpting

Focused on dimensional assets for production or visual concepting.

  • ▸
    Organic Digital Sculpting: Anatomical sculpting for creatures, characters, and organic assets.
  • ▸
    Hard Surface 3D Modeling: Mechanical props, vehicles, weapons, and architectural blockouts.
  • ▸
    Game-Ready Asset Creation: Retopology, clean UV unwrapping, and low-poly optimization.
  • ▸
    PBR Texturing & Shading: Realistic and stylized material creation for modern game engines.

Production Packages

Structured Service Packages

Structuring services into clear tiers helps clients match production budgets and timelines to concrete outputs without scope ambiguity.

TIER 01

Indie Studio Packages

— Optimized for rapid visual direction, foundational asset kits, & vertical slices.

Visual Development & Worldbuilding Kit

2–3 weeks

Best for: Pre-production pitch decks, vertical slices, and establishing art direction.

Scope & Deliverables:

  • ✓ 1 Hero visual target / keyframe illustration (lighting, tone, scale).
  • ✓ 3 Character/creature concepts with 1 final locked turnaround.
  • ✓ 2 Environment mood paintings with lighting variations.
  • ✓ Curated art guide sheet (color script, material palette, design rules).

Indie Game UI Starter Kit

1–2 weeks

Best for: Early Access launches, playable demos, and indie vertical slices.

Scope & Deliverables:

  • ✓ UX flow diagram & low-fi wireframes for 4 core screens (Main Menu, HUD, Settings, Inventory).
  • ✓ High-fidelity skinned mockups for all 4 screens in your game's visual style.
  • ✓ Icon pack of 15 essential game icons (abilities, currencies, or slots).
  • ✓ Organized Figma handoff file with engine-ready PNG/SVG assets & typography specs.
TIER 02

Commercial & Studio Production Packages

— Optimized for established pipelines, 3D integration, & marketing rollouts.

Production-Ready 3D Asset Pass

2–3 wks

Best for: Seamless handoff to 3D environment, weapon, or character art teams.

Scope & Deliverables:

  • ✓ Detailed 2D orthographic callout sheet (front, side, top, mechanics).
  • ✓ High-poly concept sculpt & optimized game-ready low-poly mesh.
  • ✓ Clean UV layout with baked 4K PBR texture maps (Albedo, Normal, Rough, Metallic).
  • ✓ Engine verification file (import-ready FBX/OBJ tested in Unreal/Unity).

Complete UI/UX Design System

4–6 wks

Best for: Complex RPGs, live-service titles, or multiplatform releases requiring scaling.

Scope & Deliverables:

  • ✓ User journey maps & interactive Figma wireframe flows.
  • ✓ Scalable UI component library (design tokens, auto-layout, variants).
  • ✓ Complete in-game HUD, nested menus, & accessibility standards doc.
  • ✓ Clickable interactive prototype with menu transition logic.

Key Art & Storefront Kit

2 wks

Best for: Steam capsule art, console storefronts, & marketing launches.

Scope & Deliverables:

  • ✓ 1 Ultra-high-res hero splash illustration (up to 8K).
  • ✓ Fully layered master PSD for video animation & trailer cutouts.
  • ✓ Multi-platform storefront resize set (Steam Header, Capsules, Socials).
