08 — PRODUCTION OUTCOME & READABILITY

Built for the Palm, Scaled for Production

Mobile top-down gameplay demands visual hierarchy that survives extreme miniaturization. Over 40 production sheets delivered instant silhouette recognition and modular asset scalability.

MOBILE READABILITY

Sub-Second Silhouette Read

Every vessel profile, hazard outline, and bullet trail evaluated in solid black before color passes to guarantee half-second recognition.

PRODUCTION SCALE

40+ Asset Delivery Suite

Delivered production sheets across capital hulls, modular sockets, bullet-hell VFX, and planetary encampments.

MODULAR EFFICIENCY

Tiered Warzone Progression

Standardized sockets and escalating debris density allowed level designers to ramp difficulty without duplicating art production costs.

DESIGN REFLECTION

“Designing for small screens forces absolute honesty: you cannot hide weak shape language behind dense textures. If a silhouette fails in half a second, the art fails gameplay.”
